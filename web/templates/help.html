Help & troubleshooting

Supported files, common problems and what to do.

Supported files

Microsoft Word .docx up to {{ max_upload_mb }} MB. Not supported: PDF, images, .doc, .docm (macros), .rtf, .odt, PowerPoint, Excel, password-protected files.

“This is a PDF. Please upload a Microsoft Word (.docx) document.”

Open the original in Word and save as .docx. Converting a PDF back to Word usually damages layout, so start from your source document.

I have a .doc file

In Word choose File → Save As → Word Document (.docx), then upload the new file.

An image on my cover page was not captioned

That is intentional: images displayed on page 1 of your original are never captioned. They are listed as “On original page 1 (protected)”.

A figure says “Page uncertain — confirm”

We could not verify which page it is on. If you are sure it is not on page 1, open it and choose Confirm not on page 1, then apply changes.

A figure says “Before first section — assign”

It appears before your first LO / Heading 1. Assign it to a section in the caption panel, or change “Figures before the first section” in Settings.

My numbering starts at the wrong LO number

By default the visible LO number is used (LO4 → Figure 4.1). Switch “LO numbering” to “Ordinal” in Settings to number sections 1, 2, 3 in order, or mark headings as ignored in Chapter mapping.

Numbers changed after I edited the document in Word

Captions use Word SEQ fields. Select all (Ctrl+A) and press F9 to update fields after adding or moving pictures. If you prefer static text, switch “Word field mode” to Static.

The preview pagination differs from Word

The preview is rendered with LibreOffice from the exact file you download. Word may break pages slightly differently when fonts differ. Captions and their positions are the same.

Images inside text boxes or SmartArt

These are listed as “Unsupported placement” and are not edited automatically, to avoid damaging layout. You can force inclusion from the caption panel; check the result in the preview.

Processing failed

The error shows what happened and a support ID. Your original is never modified. Try re-saving the document in Word and uploading again.
