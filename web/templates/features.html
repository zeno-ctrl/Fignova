Capabilities

What AutoFigure Studio does

A document engine that edits real Word structure, a review workspace that shows the true output, and AutoFigure AI for descriptions you can trust or fix.

LO-aware numbering

Detects “LO1”, “LO 2”, “Learning Outcome 3”, “LO4: Evaluation” in Heading 1 (including custom styles based on Heading 1). An “Introduction” heading before LO1 does not shift numbers. Same LO split across two headings keeps counting.

Rendered page-1 detection

Your original is rendered and each picture is located on its displayed page — not guessed from page breaks. Pictures we cannot place are held for your confirmation.

Every picture, in reading order

Inline and floating pictures, pictures in tables, VML images, grouped pictures and native charts. The same photo used twice counts as two figures.

Native Word captions

Caption style, SEQ fields with per-section restarts and correct cached numbers, a bookmark per caption, keep-with-image. Optional fully static mode.

No duplicate captions

Existing captions are detected (style, SEQ fields, text pattern). Keep and renumber, skip, or replace. Reprocessing an output never adds a second caption.

AutoFigure AI

Built-in document-context analysis reads how you introduced each figure. When the operator enables Claude and/or ChatGPT vision models, their descriptions are cross-checked; disagreements are flagged with a one-click second opinion.

True preview

Page images rendered from the exact output revision, thumbnails, zoom, fit, search, side-by-side original comparison, and caption highlights.

Versioned edits

Every applied change creates a new verified revision from your untouched original. Caption history lets you restore earlier wording.

Strict Word-only intake

Signature, package structure, macro, encryption and archive-bomb checks. PDFs, images, .doc and .docm are refused before storage.
