About AutoFigure Studio

A focused tool for one tedious job: consistent, correctly numbered figure captions in Word documents.

Students, researchers and professionals often submit documents with dozens of screenshots, diagrams and photos organised by learning outcomes. Captioning them by hand — and keeping LO1, LO2 and LO3 numbering straight — takes hours and is easy to get wrong.

AutoFigure Studio edits the real Word file instead of rebuilding it, protects the cover page, and shows you exactly what you will download.

It does not claim perfect AI or universal Word compatibility: anything uncertain is shown to you for review.
