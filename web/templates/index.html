Placement reliability

Where captions go, and when we ask you

  • Inline pictures: a Caption paragraph directly below, kept with the image.
  • Pictures in table cells: the caption stays in the same cell.
  • Floating pictures: placed after their anchor paragraph and flagged for a visual check.
  • Text boxes, SmartArt and drawing canvases are listed for review instead of being edited.
  • If a page assignment cannot be verified, the figure waits for your confirmation.

Honest FAQ

Questions people ask

Will the downloaded file look exactly like the preview?

The preview is rendered from the exact output file with LibreOffice. Microsoft Word can paginate slightly differently depending on installed fonts and version, so page breaks may shift a little. The captions, their text and positions are the same file.

Are the captions real Word captions?

Yes. By default they use the Caption style with a Word SEQ field, so they appear in a Table of Figures. Chapter numbers (the “1.” in 1.2) are written as text so the numbering is correct without changing your heading numbering. You can switch to fully static text in the settings.

What if my document has no LO headings?

We use your Heading 1 sections. With no headings at all, figures are numbered 1, 2, 3 across the document and the review screen lets you map sections manually.

Can the AI be wrong?

Yes. Suggestions are grounded in the image and your own introductory sentences, but they can still be imprecise. Low-confidence captions are flagged “needs review” and every caption is editable before download.

Do you accept PDF or .doc files?

No. Only .docx is accepted so we can edit the real Word structure. In Word use File → Save As → Word Document (.docx).

Is there a cost or account?

No account is needed. Your job is tied to a private session in this browser.
