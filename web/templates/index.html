Honest FAQ

Questions people ask

Still unsure? Read the help page.

Will the downloaded file look exactly like the preview?

The captions, their text and their positions are exactly what you download. Page breaks can differ slightly between programs, because Microsoft Word, LibreOffice and browsers lay text out a little differently.

Are the captions real Word captions?

Yes. They use Word's Caption style with a SEQ field, so they appear in a Table of Figures. You can switch to plain text in the settings.

What if my document has no LO headings?

Heading 1 sections are used instead. With no headings at all, figures are numbered 1, 2, 3 across the document, and you can map sections yourself.

Can the AI be wrong?

Yes. Suggestions are grounded in the image and your own sentences, but they can be imprecise. Uncertain captions are marked “needs review”, and every caption is editable before download.

Do you accept PDF or .doc files?

No — only .docx, so we can edit the real Word structure. In Word use File → Save As → Word Document (.docx).

Do I need an account? Is it free?

No account is needed and it is free to use. Your documents are tied to this browser; use “Open on another device” to move them.
